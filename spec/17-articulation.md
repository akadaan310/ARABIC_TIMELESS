# Layer 17 — Articulation (المخارج)

> **Band V — Execution.**

## Core statement

**The body as address space.**

Every letter has a physical location — a point in the throat and mouth at which
it is produced. The points are ordered, from deepest to most forward, and the
ordering assigns every letter an address that no convention chose.

---

## 17.1 The zones

Five zones, seventeen points (مخارج), ordered from the interior outward:

| Zone | | Letters |
|---|---|---|
| **The cavity** | الجوف | ا و ي — the letters of prolongation |
| **The throat** | الحلق | deepest: ء ه · middle: ع ح · nearest: غ خ |
| **The tongue** | اللسان | ق ك · ج ش ي · ض · ل · ن · ر · ط د ت · ص ز س · ظ ذ ث |
| **The lips** | الشفتان | ف · ب م و |
| **The nose** | الخيشوم | nasalization |

Ten of the seventeen points belong to the tongue, which is why the tongue's
letters are the most numerous and the most finely distinguished. The throat
carries three points and six letters; the lips two points and four.

---

## 17.2 The third ordering

Layer 3 gave the alphabet two orders — abjadī and hijāʾī — and showed that having
two makes every letter a coordinate pair. This layer supplies a **third**, and it
differs from the other two in kind, not merely in arrangement.

| Order | Basis | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Abjadī | inheritance | transmitted |
| Hijāʾī | kinship of shape | constructed |
| **Articulatory** | **point of production** | **discovered** |

The first two are conventions: coherent, useful, and decided. Someone arranged
them, and someone else could have arranged them differently. The third is not
decided at all. It is read off the body.

> **The body is the one substrate no era revises.**

This is the strongest timelessness argument in the architecture, and it is worth
stating without ornament. Every other layer is timeless because its methods need
no instrument. This layer is timeless because its *data* need no transmission. Two
people separated by a thousand years, with no contact, no manuscripts and no
shared teacher, derive the same table — because they are deriving it from the
same anatomy.

Sand erodes. Orders are forgotten and reconstructed differently. A mouth is a
mouth.

---

## 17.3 Operations

### Address
The point at which a letter is produced. One position per letter along the
interior-to-exterior sequence.

### Distance
How far apart two letters are produced. A **metric on the alphabet grounded in
the body** rather than in any list — and therefore a metric two strangers agree
on without having agreed on anything.

### Adjacency
Letters sharing a point, or produced at neighbouring points. Adjacency classes
are small and they cut across the shape classes entirely: ط د ت share a point and
belong to three different shape classes; ب ت ث ن ي share a shape class and are
produced at four different points.

### Comparison
The permutation relating the articulatory order to each of Layer 3's two. Like the
abjadī↔hijāʾī map, it has a cycle structure and an order, and it is tabulable by
anyone with a mouth and a list.

---

## 17.4 A fourth independent channel

The adjacency observation in §17.3 is the layer's practical contribution.

Shape class and articulation point **cross-cut**. The tooth letters ب ت ث ن ي
collapse to one shape and scatter across four points of production; the letters
ط د ت share a point and scatter across three shapes. Neither partition predicts
the other, in either direction.

So articulation is a channel independent of the skeleton, independent of weight
(which follows the counting order), and independent of profile (which follows
joining). It is the fourth such channel in the architecture, and by Layer 14's
stacking rule it is worth exactly as much as its independence — which is a great
deal.

For a reader working aloud, this channel is free: pronouncing a candidate reveals
whether its letters sit where the surrounding sounds require. That is why reading
unpointed text aloud resolves ambiguities that reading it silently does not. The
mouth is running a filter the eye cannot.

---

## 17.5 Worked example

Take the skeleton **كٮٮ** once more, and suppose the reading must alliterate or
assimilate with a neighbouring word produced at the tip of the tongue.

Of the tooth letters, ت ث ن sit at or near the tip; ب is at the lips; ي is at the
middle of the tongue. The constraint removes ب and ي from the slot immediately —
two of five, on a criterion that the skeleton cannot see, the weight cannot see,
and the profile cannot see.

The reader did nothing but say the word.

## 17.6 Contract check

| # | Condition | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Substrate independence | **Pass.** The apparatus is a mouth. No surface is required at all — this and Layer 11 are the two layers executable with nothing whatever. |
| 2 | Closure | **Pass.** Address maps letters to points; distance maps letter pairs to integers; adjacency maps letters to sets of letters. |
| 3 | Independence | **Pass.** §17.4 establishes it directly: the articulatory partition cross-cuts the shape classes, so it cannot be derived from any layer built on the written form — which is every layer below. |
| 4 | Accounted loss | **Pass.** Address is a projection whose loss is exact: it retains the point of production and discards everything about the written letter. Adjacency classes state the retained information explicitly. |
| 5 | Hand-verifiability | **Pass.** Pronounce the letter and attend to where it is made. The verification apparatus is the same one that produced the data. |

## 17.7 Relation to neighbouring layers

- **Below (Layer 3):** the third ordering, and the one that is discovered rather
  than decided. Layer 3's coordinate pair becomes a triple.
- **Below (Layer 16):** the apparatus is a body; this layer finds that the body is
  not only the instrument but also the address space.
- **Below (Layer 6):** supplies a filter available to any reader working aloud, at
  no cost beyond speaking.
- **Below (Layer 14):** a fourth independent channel, and by the stacking rule a
  valuable one — its blindnesses resemble no other layer's.
- **Forward (Layer 18):** anatomical addresses are held in the body rather than in
  memory, which makes them the cheapest storage in the architecture.
- **Forward (Layer 20):** the articulatory order is derivable from scratch by
  anyone, which makes it a true invariant and one of the load-bearing entries in
  Layer 20's list.
