# Layer 0 — The Inherited (الطبقة الموروثة)

> **Status.** Stated, not tested. This is the floor of the building, not the
> building. It is included so that what the architecture sets aside is written
> down precisely rather than gestured at.

Everything today's definition contains, compressed into one layer.

## 0.1 The letters and their skeleton

Twenty-eight letters collapse onto **fifteen** shape classes in initial and
medial position. The classes:

| Skeleton | Letters | Size |
|---|---|---|
| ٮ | ب ت ث ن ي | 5 |
| ح | ج ح خ | 3 |
| د | د ذ | 2 |
| ر | ر ز | 2 |
| س | س ش | 2 |
| ص | ص ض | 2 |
| ط | ط ظ | 2 |
| ع | ع غ | 2 |
| ٯ | ف ق | 2 |
| ا ك ل م ه و | each alone | 1 each |

Fifteen classes, twenty-eight letters, complete cover.

**The classes are position-dependent.** In final and isolated position the tooth
class splits: ن takes its own deep bowl and ي its own extended form, leaving
ٮ = {ب ت ث} at size 3. The merge is therefore not a property of a letter but of
a letter *in a place* — a fact Layer 2 takes up properly.

## 0.2 Joining

Six letters — **ا د ذ ر ز و** — accept a connection from the right and refuse
one to the left. The remaining twenty-two connect on both sides. Each letter
appears in up to four contextual forms. Layer 2 shows this is a truth table.

## 0.3 Root and pattern

A root (جذر) is a tuple of consonants, most often three. A pattern (وزن) is a
template written over the placeholders **ف ع ل**, standing for the first,
second, and third radical. Applying a pattern to a root yields a word.

The ten verb forms are the standard pattern set: فَعَلَ، فَعَّلَ، فَاعَلَ،
أَفْعَلَ، تَفَعَّلَ، تَفَاعَلَ، اِنْفَعَلَ، اِفْتَعَلَ، اِفْعَلَّ، اِسْتَفْعَلَ —
carrying causative, intensive, associative, reflexive, reciprocal, medio-passive
and requestative senses. Layers 12 and 13 restate this as a function library.

## 0.4 Values

The abjad order **أبجد هوز حطي كلمن سعفص قرشت ثخذ ضظغ** assigns to each letter a
magnitude: units 1–9, tens 10–90, hundreds 100–900, and 1000. Layer 10 takes
this up and shows what it is for.

## 0.5 Prosody

Syllabic positions are moving (متحرك) or still (ساكن). From these are built the
sabab and the watad, from those the feet (التفعيلات), and from those the sixteen
meters (البحور), generated by rotation around five circles. Layer 11 restates the
rotation as group theory.

## 0.6 Permutation of roots

The radicals of a root may be reordered; each ordering is either used (مستعمل) or
neglected (مهمل). The root space is enumerable, and its size is a plain
combinatorial fact. Layer 9 generalizes the operation off roots and onto strings.

## Why this is one layer and not the subject

Every item above describes *what the script contains*. None of them describes
what can be **done** with it, by whom, with what apparatus, at what cost, or what
survives when the apparatus changes. Layer 0 is an inventory. The twenty layers
are a system of operations over that inventory, and the operations are the point.

The inventory is also the part that is era-bound: it is transmitted, taught, and
recorded. The layers above are derivable — which is the claim Layer 20 closes on.
