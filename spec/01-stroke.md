# Layer 1 — The Stroke (الحرف كحركة)

> **Band I — The Alphabet Alone.** No words. No text. No context. Only the
> twenty-eight letters, and what a person can do with them.

## Core statement

**A letter is not a shape. It is a recorded gesture. The shape is residue.**

What survives on the surface is the trace; the letter itself is the motion that
left it. This reverses the usual order of description, and everything in this
layer follows from the reversal. A shape can only be looked at. A motion can be
*performed*, and therefore reversed, repeated, composed, timed, and counted.

---

## 1.1 The six atomic strokes

All twenty-eight letters are generated by six primitive motions:

| Stroke | | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Upright** | الألف | a single vertical descent |
| **Tooth** | السن | a short wedge — up, then down |
| **Bowl** | الحوض | a concave sweep, open upward |
| **Knot** | العقدة | a closed or near-closed loop |
| **Tail** | الذيل | an unbounded descent below the line |
| **Shoulder** | الكتف | a shallow arc riding the line |

The inventory is small on purpose. Six motions is within what a hand learns
without instruction and what a memory holds without effort — which is the first
appearance in this specification of a constraint that will keep recurring: the
apparatus is a person, so the primitives must fit a person.

## 1.2 The three operators

Strokes compose under exactly three operations:

- **Join** (وصل) — continue into the next stroke without lifting.
- **Lift** (رفع) — the hand leaves the surface; the next stroke starts fresh.
- **Return** (رجع) — retrace backward along the path just made.

Every letter is a sequence of strokes under these three operators. Nothing else
is needed, and nothing else is permitted.

---

## 1.3 Operations

### Reversal
Execute a letter's hand-path backward, end to start. Three outcomes are possible:
the letter is **self-inverse** (the reversed path retraces the same figure), it
**maps onto another letter**, or it **maps outside the alphabet** into a figure
the script does not use. Reversal therefore partitions the twenty-eight into
orbits, and the letters that fall outside are as informative as the ones that do
not — they mark where the alphabet stops.

### Mirroring
Reflect the path across the vertical axis. The script runs right to left; mirrored
wholesale, the alphabet yields a complete left-to-right ghost of itself. The
ghost is not a decoration: it is a second alphabet with the same stroke counts and
the same composition structure, and it can be written and read by anyone who can
write and read the first.

### Rotation
Quarter turns map strokes onto other strokes — an upright onto a shoulder, a bowl
onto a bowl rotated. The rotation group acting on the six primitives is small and
fully tabulable by hand.

### Stroke number
Every letter has a countable number of atomic strokes.

### Lift number
Every letter has a countable number of pen-lifts.

---

## 1.4 The consequence that matters

Stroke number and lift number are **integer-valued functions on the alphabet**.
They are derived from motion alone. They owe nothing to abjad, nothing to
position, nothing to convention, and nothing to any tradition of assigning values
to letters.

> **The alphabet is already numeric at its lowest layer — twice over — before any
> value has been assigned to any letter.**

This matters for the architecture as a whole. Layer 10 will assign magnitudes to
letters and treat strings as numbers, and that is usually presented as the moment
the script becomes computable. It is not. The script is numeric two layers
earlier, on the strength of nothing but counting how many times a hand moves and
how many times it lifts. Value is a later interpretation laid over a numeric
structure that was there from the first stroke.

---

## 1.5 Worked example

Take the upright ا. One stroke, one lift, no joins. It is the minimum: the letter
of least motion. Its stroke number is 1 and its lift number is 1, and no letter
scores lower on either. It is the alphabet's floor in both measures at once, and
it is also — by Layer 3's reckoning — the alphabet's first address in both
canonical orders and, by Layer 10's, its unit value.

That four separate layers independently single out the same letter is not a
coincidence to be admired. It is a **cross-layer agreement**, and Layer 14 is the
layer that catalogues them.

## 1.6 Contract check

| # | Condition | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Substrate independence | **Pass.** A surface, a point, a hand. A finger in sand reproduces the layer exactly. |
| 2 | Closure | **Pass.** Reversal, mirroring and rotation map letters to letters or to marked non-letters; stroke and lift number map letters to integers. |
| 3 | Independence | **Pass.** No layer below. This is the floor of the new architecture, and it carries information — the motion — that Layer 0's inventory of shapes does not record at all. |
| 4 | Accounted loss | **Pass.** Reversal, mirroring and rotation invert exactly. Stroke and lift number are projections, and their loss is stated: they discard which strokes, keeping only how many. |
| 5 | Hand-verifiability | **Pass.** Every claim is checked by making the motion and counting. No instrument, no authority. |

---

## 1.7 Relation to neighbouring layers

- **Below (Layer 0):** the inherited account describes letters as shapes to be
  recognized. This layer describes them as motions to be performed. The shape
  inventory is recoverable from the motion inventory; the reverse is not true,
  which is why this layer sits above that one.
- **Above (Layer 2):** having established what a letter *is*, Layer 2 establishes
  that a letter is never alone — that its form is a function of what stands
  beside it.
- **Forward (Layer 16):** stroke number and lift number are the first entries in
  the cost model. The Hand layer denominates every operation in the architecture
  in these units.
